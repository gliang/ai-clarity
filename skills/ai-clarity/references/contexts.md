# Lightweight context presets

These are priorities, not compulsory heading templates. Mixed answers may use section-specific guidance; do not merge unrelated scoped preferences.

| Helper context | English guidance | 中文指导 | Preserve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `general` | Supply the context needed to understand; use a concrete example only if useful. | 补充理解所需的背景；仅在有帮助时举例。 | No invented background, unnecessary instructions, or assumed expertise. |
| `research` | Clarify the question, method, evidence, and limits at the requested depth. | 说明研究问题、方法、证据及局限，深度遵从当前要求。 | Citations, equations, definitions, qualifiers, association versus causation. |
| `business` | Make the finding, assumptions, decision implications, and relevant actions explicit. | 说明发现、假设、决策影响及相关行动。 | Currency, units, reporting periods, source, estimates, uncertainty. |
| `product` | Connect user problem, proposed behavior, trade-offs, and open questions. | 关联用户问题、拟议行为、取舍与待确认问题。 | Separate observations, hypotheses, proposals, validated results. |
| `code` | Explain what the code does, why it matters, and how to verify it. | 解释代码行为、影响及验证方法。 | Exact code, commands, paths, identifiers, errors, tested versus untested status. |

Example of scope: `{"context":"code","topic":"troubleshooting"}` can hold an approved steps-first preference without affecting a conceptual research answer. A context control applies to the selected answer; only a separately approved preference affects later answers.

Traditional Chinese follows the same priorities in the requested script. Domain and language are independent: Chinese does not imply introductory depth, and English does not imply specialist knowledge.
